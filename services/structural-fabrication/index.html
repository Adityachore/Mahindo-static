About this service

Structural Fabrication Services

We fabricate structural steel for industrial and commercial buildings, from sheds and platforms to stair structures and pipe racks.

Industrial Sheds

Clear-span and multi-bay steel sheds.

Steel Buildings

Portal frames and building structures.

Platforms & Stairs

Mezzanines, platforms and staircases.

Support Structures

Pipe supports, racks and equipment frames.

Materials & quality

  • Structural steel sections (ISMB, ISMC, ISA)
  • Plates and gusset connections
  • Bolted and welded joints
  • Primer and finish coating

Applications

  • Industrial buildings
  • Warehouses
  • Workshops
  • Plant expansion
  • Infrastructure projects
